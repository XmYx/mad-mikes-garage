# Story cast reference voices

Reference clips for F5-TTS voice cloning of the story cast (audio/quest_cast). Every clip is cut from a LibriVox
dramatic reading hosted on archive.org; LibriVox recordings are dedicated to the public domain and each item below
carries a public-domain licence URL (Public Domain Mark 1.0 or CC0 1.0) in its archive.org metadata (checked by
`sources.py`). Readers are LibriVox volunteers; the clips are 6-12 s, cut at word boundaries, transcripts by
Whisper large-v3-turbo and verified (>= 90 % word match). Analysis models (WavLM-SV, audeering age/gender and
emotion) are used offline only and ship nothing. The F5-TTS weights that clone these voices are CC BY-NC 4.0:
re-voice before a commercial release.
